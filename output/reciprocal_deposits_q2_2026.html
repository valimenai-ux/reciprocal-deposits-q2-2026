Notes

Source definitions

  • Reciprocal deposits (call report). Schedule RC-E, Memorandum item 1.g "Total reciprocal deposits (as of the report date)", MDRM RCONJH83, from the FFIEC CDR bulk download "Call Reports -- Single Period" for 06/30/2026. Reciprocal deposits are deposits a bank receives through a deposit placement network in return for placing the same amount of its own customers' deposits at other network banks (section 29(i) of the FDI Act). Point in time, bank level, USD thousands as filed; summed to the holding company.
  • Brokered reciprocal deposits (call report). Schedule RC-O, item 9 "Reciprocal brokered deposits (included in Schedule RC-E, Memorandum item 1.b)", MDRM RCONG803: the part of reciprocal deposits that the bank reports as brokered because it exceeds, or the bank does not qualify for, the limited exception in 12 CFR 337.6(e). Item 9.a "Fully consolidated reciprocal brokered deposits" (RCONL190) is completed only by institutions that consolidate another insured depository institution; none of the banks in this set report it, so it is shown as not applicable rather than zero.
  • Total brokered deposits (call report). Schedule RC-E, Memorandum item 1.b "Total brokered deposits", MDRM RCON2365. Shown for context only.
  • Total deposits and total assets (call report). Total deposits = Schedule RC item 13.a deposits in domestic offices (RCON2200) plus, for FFIEC 031 filers, item 13.b deposits in foreign offices, Edge and Agreement subsidiaries and IBFs (RCFN2200). Domestic-office deposits are also carried separately. Total assets = Schedule RC item 12 (RCFD2170 for FFIEC 031 filers, RCON2170 otherwise).
  • NSFR sweep/reciprocal/brokered (row 7). Row 7 of the US NSFR public disclosure template (12 CFR 249.131(c)(1)(vii)): "Sweep deposits, brokered reciprocal deposits, and brokered deposits provided by a retail customer or counterparty". Average unweighted amounts by residual maturity (open maturity, < 6 months, 6 months to < 1 year, >= 1 year) and the average weighted amount, for the quarters ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026, taken from each holding company's semiannual NSFR disclosure PDF (file name and page number recorded in nsfr_detail). USD millions.

NSFR bundling caveat

  • Row 7 is a bundle - not a reciprocal number. The NSFR line bundles three things: affiliate and non-affiliate sweep deposits, brokered reciprocal deposits, and other brokered deposits provided by retail customers. Most of it at wealth-management firms is sweep balances. It is labelled "NSFR sweep/reciprocal/brokered (row 7)" everywhere in this repo and must never be read as reciprocal deposits. It also sits on a different basis (consolidated holding company, quarterly average) from the call report figure (insured banks, period end).

Point in time vs quarterly average

  • Call report (point in time) vs NSFR (quarterly average). Call report items are balances as of the 6/30/2026 report date. NSFR disclosure amounts are averages of daily (or, for some firms, more frequent) calculations over the quarter. The two are not directly comparable and are shown side by side only as context; no ratio in this repo mixes a call-report numerator with an NSFR denominator or vice versa.

Scope

  • Holding company vs bank level. Call report figures are summed over every active FDIC-insured depository institution whose regulatory top holder (FDIC BankFind field RSSDHCR) is the holding company. For the foreign-owned firms the top holder in FDIC data is the foreign parent (e.g. Barclays PLC, Deutsche Bank AG, The Toronto-Dominion Bank, UBS Group AG, Bank of Montreal, HSBC Holdings plc, Royal Bank of Canada); their US insured banks are attributed to the US intermediate holding company named in the task. Non-bank affiliates and uninsured trust companies are outside the call report figures. NSFR disclosures are consolidated at the (intermediate) holding company and include non-bank subsidiaries.
  • Structure verified via FDIC BankFind rather than NIC. The subsidiary list and RSSD IDs were verified against the FDIC BankFind Suite institutions API (fields FED_RSSD, RSSDHCR, NAMEHCR; index of 23 October 2026), not against the FFIEC National Information Center, because NIC serves a CAPTCHA challenge to automated clients. The two draw on the same Federal Reserve structure data, but NIC itself was not consulted.

Confidentiality change

  • RC-O items 9 and 9.a become confidential from the 9/30/2026 report date. The Federal Reserve MDRM data dictionary (downloaded 27 October 2026) carries two entries for RCONG803 and RCONL190: public (Confidentiality = N) through the 6/30/2026 report date and confidential (Confidentiality = Y) from 9/30/2026 onward. Schedule RC-E Memorandum item 1.g (RCONJH83) stays public. The 6/30/2026 report is therefore the last public quarter for the brokered-reciprocal split used here.

Firms excluded, not required, or not yet filed

  • DWS USA Corp: no FDIC-insured depository subsidiary in FDIC BankFind, so no call report figures; shown in the table for completeness.
